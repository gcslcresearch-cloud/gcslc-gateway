## Principles

1. **Generative over static** — The interface is driven by the Eagle: sniffing, striking, and swarm logic. No passive emoji bubbles; every node is discovered by the Apex Predator.
2. **8R in the loop** — D1 Refine, D2 Reset, D3 Research (and D4–D8) are reflected in UI state: the Eagle’s strike applies determinants; the swarm represents sovereign coverage.
3. **Sovereign Glass** — Deep navy, gold (#FFD700), instrumentation-style counters. The Eagle is the single source of truth for $10B+ opportunities.
4. **One anchor** — $170.85B valuation anchor is the central metric. The Generative Eagle surfaces gaps; the UI never obscures the anchor.

## Generative Logic (Apex Engine v4.0)

- **Sniffing:** When not striking, the Eagle rotates and bobs (sin/cos on `elapsedTime`). Autonomous 60s sniff highlights $10B+ nodes.
- **Strike:** On node selection or autonomous trigger, Talon Strike runs; play_swat (180 Hz → 40 Hz); D1/D2/D3 word-pop and Dynamic Brief.
- **Swarm:** Instanced mini-eagles (Samsung Good Lock–inspired) scale sovereign coverage; optional overlay for multi-node view.
- **Eye-contact:** After landing, Eagle locks gaze; TTS: *"I have identified the gap. The anchor is secured. Get to work."*
